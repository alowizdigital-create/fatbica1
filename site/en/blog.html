The Dean's Blog

Reflections, news and teaching from the Dean of FATBICAM.

Simon MVONDO, Dean of FATBICAM

Welcome to this space, which I want to devote to sharing: biblical reflections, campus news, and a look into the life of our community in Yaoundé.

Training servants of God is daily work, made up of classes, prayer and personal mentoring. It is this everyday life that I want to share with you here.

You will also find topics close to our heart as a faculty: faithfulness to Scripture, practical training for ministry, and the community life that shapes our students.

I invite you to visit regularly, and to pray with us for the work that continues at FATBICAM.
