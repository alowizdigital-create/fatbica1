Discover FATBICAM

A theological community rooted in the Word, serving the Church in Africa and around the world.

Our History

The Cameroon Biblical Theology Faculty (FATBICAM) trains men and women for service to the Church, founded on the conviction that Scripture alone is the authority for faith and practice.

From our campus in Yaoundé, we welcome students from across Cameroon and beyond, eager to deepen their knowledge of Scripture and prepare for pastoral, missionary or community ministry.

Our teaching is built on a rigorous, systematic study of the Bible, in keeping with its authority and inerrancy, delivered by committed and accessible teachers.

Beyond the classroom, FATBICAM is a community of life: students pray, serve and grow together, in a family spirit that shapes their journey long after they leave.

Our Mission

To train men and women faithful to Scripture, well equipped for pastoral ministry, biblical teaching, and service to the Church in Africa and beyond.

Our Vision

To see strong churches, faithful to the Word, led by well-trained workers, in every region of Cameroon and beyond its borders.

Statement of Faith

What we believe, grounded in the Holy Scriptures.

1

The Holy Scriptures

We believe that the Bible, in its sixty-six books, is the inspired, infallible and inerrant Word of God in the original manuscripts. It is the sole and sufficient authority for faith and conduct.

2

The Triune God

We believe in one God, eternally existing in three distinct persons — the Father, the Son and the Holy Spirit — equal in power and glory.

3

Jesus Christ

We believe in the full deity and full humanity of Jesus Christ, born of the virgin Mary, who died on the cross for our sins, rose bodily, and is seated at the right hand of the Father.

4

The Holy Spirit

We believe that the Holy Spirit convicts the world of sin, regenerates the repentant sinner, and permanently indwells every believer, enabling them to live a holy life and serve effectively.

5

Man and Sin

We believe that man was created in the image of God, but that through Adam's disobedience, all people are born sinners and separated from God, unable to save themselves.

6

Salvation

We believe that salvation is a gift of God's grace, received through faith alone in the finished work of Jesus Christ, and not by works.

7

The Church

We believe that the Church, the body of Christ, is made up of all born-again believers, distinct from Israel, and is called to worship, fellowship and the proclamation of the Gospel.

8

Things to Come

We believe in the personal and visible return of Jesus Christ, the resurrection of the dead, and the final judgment, when everyone will give an account before God.
