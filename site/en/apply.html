Apply

The best time to start is now. Our team supports you at every step of your application.

General Information:

Application deadline for the September 2027 intake: July 31, 2027.

Submitting your application

The following items should be added to your file as soon as possible (send to candidature@fatbicam.org):

  • Copy of your ID card or residence permit,
  • Copy of all your diplomas,
  • 1 digital photo of you with your family,
  • A medical certificate confirming your fitness for study,
  • Your testimony of conversion to the Christian faith,
  • A description of your involvement in your church or a Christian ministry,
  • Have you received a call to ministry? If so, how, and for what ministry?
  • Why do you want to study at FATBICAM?

Download the information sheet:

FATBICAM information sheet.pdf

Admission Requirements

For undergraduate level (Bachelor's)

  • Be born again and testify to an active Christian life,
  • Hold a high school diploma, GCE A-Level or equivalent,
  • Provide a letter of recommendation from your local church,
  • Be at least 18 years old at the start of the academic year.

For graduate and doctoral levels

  • Hold the diploma of the previous level (Bachelor's for the Licence, Licence for the Master's),
  • Be actively serving or preparing for ministry within a church,
  • Provide a letter of recommendation from your church or spiritual leader,
  • Successfully complete an admission interview with the academic leadership.

Academic Calendar

September 2027 Intake

  • Application deadline: July 31, 2027
  • Admission interviews: August 2027
  • Academic year opening and welcome for new students: first week of September 2027
  • Classes begin: second week of September 2027

Academic Year Structure

  • First semester: September to December
  • End-of-semester exams: December
  • Second semester: January to May
  • Final exams and graduation: June
