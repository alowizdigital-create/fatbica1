Support FATBICAM

Your support helps train workers for the Kingdom and maintain the Yaoundé campus. Thank you for joining this work.

Training a future pastor, teacher or church servant comes at a cost: housing, meals, the library, teachers' salaries and the upkeep of the Yaoundé campus.

Many students come from modest families and cannot finance their studies alone. Your gift allows them to devote themselves fully to their training.

Every contribution, one-time or recurring, has a direct impact on campus life and on the future of the churches our graduates will serve.

FATBICAM operates thanks to the generosity of partners in Africa and around the world who share our conviction in the importance of solid biblical training.
