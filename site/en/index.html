OUR CAMPUS

A place to grow together

Studying theology at FATBICAM is far more than an academic curriculum. It means choosing a community. On our campus in Yaoundé, you live, pray, discuss and grow alongside your professors and fellow students.

We don't only train theologians: we accompany men and women called to serve churches in Africa and around the world.
