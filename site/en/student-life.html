Student Life

Studying at FATBICAM also means living a rich community experience, between studies, service and spiritual life.

Beyond the classroom, FATBICAM students share times of prayer, meals and moments of relaxation that build lasting bonds.

Many of our students are already active in a local church, where they put into practice what they learn on campus.

The Yaoundé campus offers an environment well suited to student life: common areas, a library, gardens and shared facilities.

Each class develops its own group dynamic, carried by the year's clubs and spiritual activities.

Student Associations

Student Association

Represents students to the administration and organizes campus community life.

Worship Team

Leads chapel services and campus worship, and performs at the faculty's major events.

Prayer Cells

Small groups of students who meet regularly to pray for one another and for God's work.

Spiritual Life

Daily Chapel

A time of worship and biblical teaching shared by the whole campus community every day.

Prayer Weeks

Special times set aside for prayer and seeking God, held several times during the academic year.

Church Practicums

Every student is encouraged to serve regularly in a local church throughout their studies.
