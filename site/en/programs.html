Our Programs

From certificate to doctoral level, FATBICAM offers full or à la carte programs for every calling and every season of life.

All our programs are built on a rigorous, systematic study of the Holy Scriptures, combined with solid pastoral and practical training.

Each level deepens knowledge of the Bible, systematic theology, Church history and the skills needed for ministry: preaching, teaching, biblical counseling and church leadership.

Our programs are available on our Yaoundé campus through day classes, or through our extension courses for students who cannot reside on campus.

The same academic standards and the same supervision apply to every student, whatever program they choose.
